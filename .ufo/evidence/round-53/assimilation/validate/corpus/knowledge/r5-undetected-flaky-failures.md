## The mechanism, in the source's own terms

Observation, from quotes 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8: the abstract says modern CI systems commonly rerun failed tests **within the same job** to identify flaky failures (2); that apparently deterministic failures were validated through reruns **in fresh environments inside the projects' original CI infrastructure** (4); that undetected flaky failures are 9.8 %–16.3 % of failed pipeline runs (5); that failures concentrate heavily in small test case subsets (7); and that the most disruptive undetected cases remain largely invisible to tools relying on current detection mechanisms (8). Inference: the abstract does not say that the four studied projects' own detector was the same-job rerun; it says that mechanism is the common one.

Observation, from quote 1: the definition of the phenomenon is a test whose outcome varies "under conditions that developers intend to maintain constant" — the intent to hold conditions constant is part of the definition, not an assumption about them.

Observation, from quote 10: the paper ships a dataset of 154,000 flaky test cases and 1.7 million flaky failures.

## What this adds to F3

The ground F3 already has behind it (the frontier row stays `open`) is a repeated-control precondition with a finite sample: `corpus/README.md:127` records the scope of `knowledge/repeated-control-precondition.md` ("A standalone exact-tuple repeated-control gate; a clean result is finite sample agreement, not proof of determinism or a flaky-test cure"), and `corpus/README.md:80` states the next move as "N ≥ 3 byte-identical controls, or a named mismatch". The lane's F5 entry already refuses the stronger claim (`corpus/knowledge/hermetic-env-differential.md:4`: F3 universal determinism and successor freshness remain unclaimed).

- **The lane's repeat gate repeats inside one environment.** `tools/repeat-control.py:18` says the run mode executes the argv N times with "the environment is inherited unchanged", and `:94` says the clean verdict "is not a determinism proof and it cures no flakiness". Inference: N byte-identical controls therefore establish stability *in the inherited environment*; quote 2 is the name of the mechanism whose blind spot that sampling design inherits, and quote 4 is the design that does not.
- **A fresh-environment rerun is the precondition the lane can now state with a size attached.** Inference, a proposal the frontier table has not adopted (`corpus/README.md:80` still prints the old next move): F3's next move could become "N ≥ 3 controls that agree while the environment tuple varies" — because the source's undetected-flaky verdicts were produced by reruns in fresh environments (4), and the failures those reruns identified as undetected flaky failures are 9.8 %–16.3 % of failed pipeline runs (5), i.e. a same-job verdict would have called every one of them a real failure.
- **The miss is systematic, not incidental.** Inference: quote 3 says the existing literature predominantly analyzes data from the same-job mechanism, and quote 8 says the most disruptive undetected cases stay invisible to tools relying on it; read together, a lane that adopts a same-job rerun as its flaky oracle is not using a weak detector but the detector the paper measures as blind to its own worst class.
- **The concentration matters for fixture design.** Inference: quote 7 says failures concentrate heavily in small test case subsets, so a suite-level flake rate hides the per-case distribution; an F3 verdict stated per suite can be clean while the few cases that decide the verdict are the unstable ones.

## What this adds to F5

- **Environment is a first-class flake input.** Quote 9 states up to 3× variation in flake rates between environments. The lane's F5 experiment (`corpus/knowledge/hermetic-env-differential.md:1-6`) varies one declared input across seven profiles over nine pinned surfaces and reports them clean. Inference: the two results are consistent and do not generalise into each other — the lane's *gates* were insensitive to the inputs it varied, while the source's *real suites* vary up to 3× between environments; the lane must not read its own gates' insensitivity as a property of the suites it verifies.
- **The mechanism for the F3 precondition already exists in the lane.** Inference: `tools/env-diff.py` (the contract and `--selftest` forms named at `corpus/knowledge/hermetic-env-differential.md:6`) is the instrument that can vary the environment tuple across a repeated control; it differs from the protocol quote 4 describes in two ways — the source reruns in a replacement environment where the instrument varies one declared input across profiles, and the source applies the rerun to an apparently deterministic failure where a repeated control runs on a green case — wiring it into `tools/repeat-control.py`'s N runs is the concrete F3 move, and its falsifier is a pair of controls that agree in one environment and disagree across two.
