## Bounded gate recommendation (one)

Concretely: `ProfileBashTsukuroiCensus` counts toward dead (37) despite its existing report + surface evidence; `ProfileBashMadoVerify` counts dead with no report; `ProfileBashChecklistRun` counts completed only because its retained callback says `completed`.
