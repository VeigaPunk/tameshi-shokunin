## Contradictions and limits

Within the requested identity/status/role population, no callback-to-outcome or role-to-membership contradiction was found: the two completed callbacks project to completed rows with their named reports; the one failed callback projects to failed with no assigned report. The three-seat slice therefore has two completed and one failed, all with `replaced: false`.

Two metadata details should not be mistaken for seat-status contradictions: the failed outcome row says `provider: codex` while its `resolved_model` is `zai/glm-5.3-flash:max` and its callback error names Z.AI; semantics for that `provider` field are not established by the reviewed contract. Also, registry top-level `wave` is `null`, whereas `spec2.json` names `tameshi-r21-w2`; the registry’s `.reportDir` and all three member identities still point to the expected wave-2 population. Neither discrepancy changes the observed per-seat callback result. The round-22 accounting contract and `tools/wave-accounting.py` explicitly limit authority to caller-declared, digest-pinned retained inputs; they cannot authenticate provider provenance.
