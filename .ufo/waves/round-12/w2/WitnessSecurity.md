## Axes
1. **Write confinement**: the tool writes only to its own evidence directory and to temp.
2. **Sibling read-only**: nothing is written to kagi or to the host's real surfaces.
3. **Frozen pin execution**: rev, PIN and the runner are bound, and the pin is enforced at execution.
4. **Scratch hygiene**: temp is cleaned up, and evidence is retained, not clobbered.

## What held (observed)
- **Sibling read-only.** `git -C ../kagi-shokunin status --porcelain` printed 0 lines after WitnessFinish's sweep.
- **No textconv execution.** Kagi has no repo-local textconv, filter or fsmonitor config, and no `.gitattributes`; global config has only LFS filters and aliases. In a scratch probe (P2), a blob read with `git show <rev>:path` that had `diff=evil` attributes did **not** run the textconv (git 2.55.0).
- **Pin binding at execution.** In the retained crashed-sweep log, `CONTROL: GREEN … pristine-sha256=275d8294…53009f` and `MUTATE_RESTORE: OK before=after=275d8294…` both equal `PIN_BASH` (`mutate-and-run.sh:544`). That log's `.cli.exit` is `0`. The declaration records rev `fecee874…a96e` (a commit in kagi), pin = target sha, and runner sha `75ed934d…`.
- **Runner writes stay in the workdir.** The crashed sweep directory holds only `defects.tsv`, `evidence/`, `mutant-B*.pristine.sh`, `pristine.pristine.sh`, `results.tsv` and `spec.copy`. These match the runner's write sites (`mutate-and-run.sh:423-455,478,493`).
- **Spec mutations do not redirect fixture roots.** None of the 10 `@old/@new` edits touch `resolve`, `ROOT`, `HOME`, the traps or `mktemp`. The selftest verify path is `--root "$R/host"` (`pristine.sh:760-763`), so mutants cannot reach real `$HOME`.
- **Temp cleanup.** For the sweep window (00:46:00–00:47:00), `find /tmp -maxdepth 1` showed no `tmp.*`, `surface-census.*`, `bash-witness-selftest.*` or `tameshi-r4-2-*` entries. The suite cleans `R` through its EXIT trap (`pristine.sh:698`), and child `verify` runs keep their own `cleanup` trap (`:113-119`). The tool's own selftest uses `mkdtemp` with `rmtree` in `finally` (`:977`, `:1119`).
- **Crash path.** At sha `dbfc34d1`, `KeyError: 'skip'` (`run_problems` with `control_record`'s `skip_labels`) aborted after the sweep. No summary was written, so no false count resulted. The raw sweep was retained under a `crashed-…` name. The current sha patches this at `:562`. Parser correctness belongs to WitnessParser and WitnessIntegrity.
