- **G3: the porcelain check cannot attribute writes on a live fleet.** "The sibling tree stays porcelain-clean" has no record for the mado round in either cited file. As an executable check it also fails to separate the service from other writers:
  - The grounding sibling (`mado-shokunin`) was dirty before the run.
  - Concurrent lanes changed it during the run.
  - The service could not write at all.

  A reader who compares porcelain snapshots gets a false alarm in this situation, and could equally miss a real write hidden among concurrent changes. What separates the two is write confinement (a read-only mount, or byte-copy/git-object materialization) plus the target restore hash. One possible wording: "porcelain unchanged by the run's own writes, proven by confinement".
- **G4: unanchored reference.** "Matrix row F" is the only reference in the section without a file:line anchor. It resolves to `arm-class-matrix.md:206-215` only through two hops in the census.
