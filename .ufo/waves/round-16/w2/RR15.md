artifact itself is forward-declared. Running `python3 assemble.py --skill SKILL.md` in /tmp exits 1
("Exits 1 when any section lacks an entry") and writes a ledger with the section list, my range

minor; **it becomes blocking if round-16 closes without committing `ledger.json`**, since box 3's
