## Findings
**F1 — minor, concrete, fixable: frontmatter is not strict-YAML parseable (lines 1–4).**
The unquoted `Rank: shonen (probation).` inside the `description:` plain scalar introduces a second `: `, which strict YAML reads as a nested mapping and rejects. Reproduced on two independent implementations (PyYAML 6.0.3; Ruby Psych 3.4.10). A tolerant line-based consumer still gets a description string (it will contain `Rank: …`), so the blast radius depends on the consumer; omp CLI launch is forbidden to me, so I did not verify which parser the skill loader uses. Minimal fix that parses cleanly: `description: "Domain specialist — verification-fixture craft. Rank: shonen (probation)."` (verified OK), or move rank to its own frontmatter key. Separately, the rank value `shonen (probation)` cannot be re-checked here because `SHONEN.md` was modified in this same commit but is out of scope.

**F2 — minor, ambiguity/staleness risk: "Two mutation cases are now measured" reads as a census (line 9).**
At path-name level the same commit (a) claims to land `js-round-gate case 7`, (b) adds a corpus document literally named `mutation-case-kumitate-round-gate.md`, and (c) lands 10 named case outputs plus a control under `round-gate/sweep/evidence/`. Read literally — "two mutation cases are now measured" — a cold reader concludes the corpus holds exactly two measured mutation cases, which the same-commit artifact names contradict. I could not open the contents (corpus knowledge and evidence bodies are outside my allowed reads), so I do not assert the contents contradict the sentence; I assert the sentence is at best scoped to "the two cases reproduced in Appendix A/B" and should say so, because the round-16 full-page cold read is exactly the audience that will take the census at face value. Suggested wording: "Two cases carry complete reproduce specs here — round 2 JS, round 3 bash, both 8/10 killed (Appendices A/B); further cases live in corpus/knowledge/."
