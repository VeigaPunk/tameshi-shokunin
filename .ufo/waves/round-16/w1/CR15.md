1. **The record is honest about drift.** The I1 incident (`:17-23`) is exactly what a reader can

   smoothing occurred.
2. **Ephemeral receipts are the weak seam of an otherwise replayable method.** Both the mado
   evidence tree and the audit receipt lived under `/tmp`; a reader a week later can re-run the
   control (I did: 28/28, exit 0) but cannot re-open a single probe reading or the audit lane's
   pres-sweep. For a method whose product is a receipt, the audit receipt should be copied into
   the corpus (or explicitly labelled ephemeral).

   `/tmp/w5-mado-m52-selftest.*` directories appeared (mtimes 2026-10-20 10:26:25–10:27:24
   -0300), from a concurrent lane running the same gate; my sandbox leaked nothing
   (`before=17 after=17`, private tmpfs). I did not read, modify or remove any of them. This is a
   live instance of the note's own U4 hazard.
4. **No wrong expectation was observed.** Every headline number in the record either recomputes
   or reproduces; the two problems are citation/traceability, not measurement.
