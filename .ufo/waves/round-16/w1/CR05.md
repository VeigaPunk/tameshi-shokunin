## Findings by severity

**Medium — rule 2's justification is false (246-272).** The two-space substring replacement
produces the identical mutant sha (`1ea6d77f…`), not "a different mutant and different bytes".
Reader impact: someone tuning a sweep will look for a byte-divergence risk that this example does
not exhibit, and may distrust the (correct) rest of the rule. Repair: state the real mechanism —
the two-space match begins at offset 4 of the same physical line, so the uniqueness gate cannot
distinguish it and the edit site is ambiguous to a human reviewer; include the full line so the
manifest and the artifact agree byte-for-byte. If a differing-bytes example is wanted, use an
EMPTY-`new` deletion (measured above: 895 vs 896 newlines), which genuinely depends on the
trailing bytes.

**Medium — rule 4 cites a token absent from both manifests (276-282).** `${n}`: 0 occurrences in
`mutation-case-kimi-census.md` and `mutation-case-kagi-census.md`. The other three tokens are
present exactly as cited. Repair: replace `${n}` with `${#value}` (kagi.md:227, the B6 manifest)
or delete it; the literal-replacement argument is unaffected.

**Low — catalog row "weakened type/kind pin" states the inverse edit shape (305).** Row says
"broaden a kind check"; the cited worked instance narrows it: `isLeaf(kind)` at
kimi-census.mjs:292, where `isLeaf` is `kind !== 'absent' && kind !== 'dir'` (:205), is replaced by
`kind === 'file'` — accept-set shrinks. The kill mechanism ("anti-read fixtures") is correct
(kimi:72-73). Repair: relabel the shape as narrowing (`isLeaf → file-only`) or mark the cell as
"inverse of the catalogued shape; no broadening instance measured".

**Low — D5 is counted in two class rows (298-299).** It appears under "dropped arm" and
"weakened presence gate", but the edit at kimi-census.mjs:255 (`gens.length > 0` → `true`) deletes
no lines; only the presence-gate row matches the shape. Repair: drop D5 from the dropped-arm cell
(leave `D4 → 3`), preserving the same measured total.

**Low — "One defect per class per sweep" contradicts its own calibration (248-250).** Both worked
sweeps inject 10 defects over fewer classes: round 2's tally is `off-by-one 1/2 · … dropped arm
2/2` (8 classes, kimi:55-57); round 3's is `dropped branch 1/2` (9 classes, kagi:78-80). Repair:
restate as "at least one defect per class; a second defect in the same class is allowed when the
class has two distinct mechanisms, and the tally records both" — that matches what the two
calibration sweeps actually did.

**Low — unresolvable references (267, 277).** "recipe step 5" lies outside the assigned range and
could not be read; "both drivers" is unnamed in the section and in the notes beyond
