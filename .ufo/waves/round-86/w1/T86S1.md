## Findings

**Defect M1 (inherited, confirmed at the bytes).** Round-85's certification freeze carried no
criterion-111 cheat family: at `118dbc80` the round-85 evidence tree holds one file and no
`controls/` directory, and the r85 plan names no fingerprint/sequence/pinned-hash control.
Criterion 111 stood before that freeze. Round-86 pays this: five controls, all below target,
committed before the freeze, and the r85 witness carried byte-exact. This is the round-85
inherited miss the proposal names, and it is a **miss, not a pass** — a later round cannot erase
it by having executed the family.

**Defect M2 (this round's own artifacts — off-by-one pointers).** The frozen plan and contract
carry "page lines 1807"/"1811"; the quotes are at 1808/1812. Lines 1807/1811 are `<dd>` tags that
render empty. Red-before receipt: the fixture at 1807/1811 → `resolved=0/2`, exit 1; green at
1808/1812 → `resolved=2/2`, exit 0. L1 has corrected the plan and contract **in the working
tree only**; `corpus/SOURCES.md:713-714` still says "page lines 1807 and 1811" and must be
corrected before landing, otherwise the registry's own note points at empty lines.

**Defect M3 (this round's own artifacts — source over-reach).** The lesson's Gate attributes to
S305 the classification of a declared tool path "as a regular blob". The page enumerates no
octal modes; the 100644/100755 boundary is S291's ground. Narrow the sentence and name S291
beside S305.

**Defect M4 (this round's own artifacts — quote word count).** The second quote is 9 whitespace
tokens / 9 alphabetic words, not 10. The first quote's "5 words" is correct under every
convention measured. The precedent is criterion 116's own r59 finding (S289: recorded 17, actual
18).

**Fixture-craft note.** A grep-only pointer checker reports the second quote **absent** (it is
not a literal byte substring) and the first present; a checker that renders the HTML reports both
present. A pointer fixture for this source must render tags and unescape entities, and must
assert on rendered text — the raw-substring property is not stable across the two quotes and
should not be the assertion.
