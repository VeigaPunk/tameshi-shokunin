## Findings

- F1 (C05, substring citation): with `path in token`, a pin is cited by any substring of a backticked token. Fixture (scratch, committed only in the fixture): doc row cites `` `r/a.txt` ``; manifest pins historical `a.txt` at a rev where it exists. Frozen: `CAP2_ROW alpha disposition=historical bound=no reason=pin-not-cited`, exit 1. C05: `bound=yes reason=pin:a.txt@…`, exit 0. The frozen selftest has no such case, so the gap is not caught by its own selftest. Real and overreach populations do not reach it.
- F2 (C12, whole-word rule): the selftest does not kill the removal of the whole-word-in-command rule. The real population does (30 rows become 20 bound, 10 unbound). The selftest lacks a case where a pin is cited only as a bare word inside a command.
- F3 (C20, zero table rows): with the zero-rows refusal removed, a table with a header and separator but no rows is accepted: `CAP_CENSUS_V2: rows=0 bound=0 unbound=0 mismatches=0`, exit 0. Frozen: `capability-census-v2: setup refusal zero-table-rows`, exit 2. Not covered by the selftest.
- F4 (K01, stderr): removing the refusal-stderr conjunct leaves the real tool at 8/8, but ctl-r85 reaches 6/8 and ctl-cn1 3/8. The stderr check is doing work the other checks do not cover for these controls. It is not beaten to 8/8, so it counts as survived under the literal rule.
- F5 (K06, PATH pin): nothing in the eight cases exercises a PATH-shadowed git or child tool, so the pin's protection is untested by the frozen counter.
- F6 (C07): the filesystem-manifest mutant crashes the census selftest with an uncaught `FileNotFoundError` instead of a setup refusal. This is counted as killed under the kill rule, but the selftest does not guard the absent-file case in the non-git path. Not a survivor, recorded for completeness.
