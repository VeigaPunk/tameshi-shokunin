and the frozen bytes stay pinned and unedited (restoration evidence is the

expectation (controls-v93.grade: exit code plus the state's named conjunct) must

committed state exercises the rule (or the pristine baseline fails). A
SURVIVED mutant is a coverage finding about the battery, not permission to change
the contract. "delta" is the raw-output change a surviving mutant still makes in
a committed receipt that no graded expectation looks at.
