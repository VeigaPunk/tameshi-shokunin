## 3. Why the selftest is load-bearing: the live sweep does not catch four of five

The same five mutants run through the **real 17-row sweep** on the base
`SCORECARD.md` at `21ef81e9` (`candidates/manifest17.json`: 16 live capability
rows + `r74-frozen-addr-six`). E2's candidate, three runs each:

| instrument | rows | match | stale | unexpected | exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E2 candidate (control) | 17 | 9 | 8 | **7** | 1 |
| a echo | 17 | 17 | **0** | 1 | 1 |
| b expect ignored | 17 | 9 | 8 | **0** | **0** |
| c capability absent → match | 17 | 9 | 8 | 7 | 1 |
| d zero rows success | 17 | 9 | 8 | 7 | 1 |
| e doc-rev ignored | 17 | 9 | 8 | 7 | 1 |

My stub produced the identical table on the same manifest. Consequences:

- **(a) is caught by the live sweep only because of the frozen row.** Echoing
  the doc makes every row `match`; the frozen row's `expect: stale` turns
  `unexpected` to 1. Drop the frozen row and the echo cheat reads
  `stale=0 unexpected=0` exit 0 (the plan's control 2 says exactly this).
- **(b) makes the base state look like the target.** With `expect` ignored the
  base doc reads `unexpected=0` exit 0 while the honest instrument reads
  `unexpected=7` exit 1. No live-sweep predicate distinguishes them.
- **(c) is inert on the intact manifest but turns kill rule (ii) into a pass.**
  With a mangled capability name (`renamed17.json`) the honest E2 candidate
  refuses and mutant (c) emits a full sweep with the mangled row silently
  counted `match`:
  ```
  $ <E2> --repo <lane> --manifest renamed17.json --doc-rev 21ef81e9
  SELFSTATE error=setup capability-absent:r-receipt-exists:receipt-existence sweepX
  exit=2
  $ e2_c_capability_absent_match.py ... same args
  SELFSTATE: rows=17 match=9 stale=8 unexpected=7
  exit=1
  ```
  An unmeasured row that still counts. (Stub-measured: honest
  `SETUP capability-absent:r-receipt-exists`; mutant `rows=17 match=9 stale=8
  unexpected=7`.)
- **(d) is inert on a non-empty manifest;** on `{"rows": []}` the honest
  instrument refuses `zero-rows`, mutant (d) reports
  `rows=0 match=0 stale=0 unexpected=0` exit 0.
- **(e) is inert whenever worktree == `--doc-rev`** (true at base and at the
  landing tip). It corrupts any historical re-derivation (§1.1).

**Finding F2:** the frozen plan specifies no `--selftest` for C2 at all. E2's
candidate supplies nine arms (good), but the plan's kill rules do not require
them; a candidate without a selftest would satisfy the after-state while
carrying (b)–(e). The minimum committed arm set for the five behaviours is the
stub's five arms; E2 needs only the sixth (`--doc-rev`).
