## Axes named

- **Calibration axis as judged in the lane** (`corpus/QUALITY.md` criteria
  78-83 and 141): a record-repair counter binds *what the repair does*, not
  that tokens exist. QUALITY 141: "a registry row's argv equals the declared
  entry command and the tool's blob equals its pin before the row's result
  counts; a correction is bound by its operative sentence, not by a phrase that
  a quoted rejection also contains; the checker tools are run from pinned
  blobs, not from the candidate tree."
- **Precedent axis, rounds 81-82**: criterion 78 (a routed-defect register is a
  measured population) and criterion 141's admitted falsifiers
  (`T90C2-forge-registry.out`, `T90C2-wrong-count.out`,
  `T90C2-tool-neuter.out` — each prints `C2_R90_PASS=true`). The round-82
  hardening cited in this very claim (`GIT_NO_REPLACE_OBJECTS`, manifest
  pre-bind) is a *precedent that token/bytes sealing without operative-sentence
  binding is bookkeeping*, not the calibration move itself.
- **Non-author-routed-defect axis** (`round-90-verifier-spotcheck.md` §6
  items 1-2): item 1 = the case note carries no "not admitted" marker;
  item 2 = the entry must cite its coverage-map row by an appended note
  ("so a reader of the entry alone sees the kill"). Both are *reader-facing*
  repairs: they succeed iff the entry, read alone, shows the admission/citation.

## Frontier movement

Do-nothing is not the bar here. `T91C2-nowork.out` — the counter run against
the frozen lane at `50e59d59`, untouched — prints

```
DEFECT_BIND_ROW … state=open (all three rows, section-absent)
C2_SUMMARY … rows=fail argv=ok toolpin=ok blobrun=ok defect_bind_exit=1
C2_R91_PASS=false
exit=1
```

i.e. the current PASS condition is *reachable only by appending the tokens*,
not by the do-nothing state, but the honest state is also reachable while the
reader-facing defect is still present. That is the exact gap between a
bookkeeping closure (rows closed) and a calibration closure (repair operative).
QUALITY 141 names the gap explicitly: "bound by its operative sentence, not by
a phrase that a quoted rejection also contains."

## Kill attempts (kill rule a)

Harness `T91C2-forge.py` clones the lane at the freeze rev and appends one of
three note sections (the lesson addendum is the honest fixes-v91.py text in
every variant, isolating items 1-2), commits in the clone only, and runs the
frozen counter with the clone as lane and the forge commit as REV.

- **variant reject** (`T91C2-reject.out`, `clone_rev=760c4ee2…`): the appended
  "Admission status" section carries all five tokens (`This row carries
  **not admitted**`, `killed by its own frozen`, `corpus/README.md`,
  `Round 90 — coverage map: …`) inside sentences that *quote them as rejected
  readings* and in operative sentences that say the entry does NOT carry the
  admission and does NOT cite the row as a back-citation. A non-author reader
  of the entry sees the round-90 defect preserved verbatim in intent.
- **variant falsereason** (`T91C2-falsereason.out`, `clone_rev=d140f2e5…`):
  an affirmative marker whose operative sentence states a *false* kill reason
  (rule (b) rather than the true rule (a)), and names the coverage-map row in
  a sentence that is not a back-citation. Also unrepaired for a reader who
  knows the record (the counter was killed by rule (a), `refs/replace` +
  ambiguous-row skip, not by a divergent non-author execution).
- **variant pos** (`T91C2-pos.out`, `clone_rev=10483bce…`): honest
  fixes-v91.py `NOTE_APPEND` — positive control.

Observed:

```
reject:      DEFECT_BIND rows=3 closed=3 open=0 unexpected=0, exit 0 → C2_R91_PASS=true, exit=0
falsereason: DEFECT_BIND rows=3 closed=3 open=0 unexpected=0, exit 0 → C2_R91_PASS=true, exit=0
pos:         DEFECT_BIND rows=3 closed=3 open=0 unexpected=0, exit 0 → C2_R91_PASS=true, exit=0
```

All three states are *operatively different* to a non-author reader, yet
indistinguishable to the counter. Kill rule (a) requires "a committed state
reaches C2_R91_PASS=true while a non-author reading finds item 1 or 2 not
actually repaired" — **both reject and falsereason satisfy it**. The
pre-freeze `C2-wrongcount` / `C2-keepred` controls show the counter refuses
only the two *naive* corruptions (false count token and missing operative
token); the QUALITY-141-specific corruption — required tokens present but
only as a *quoted rejection* — is not among them and is not refused.

Root cause: `tools/defect-bind.py` `check_doc_contains` (:336-348) is a
substring check (`t not in body`) over the section body. QUALITY 141 says the
correction "is bound by its operative sentence, not by a phrase that a quoted
rejection also contains", but the manifest's `doc-contains` kind cannot
express operative-sentence binding; the C2 counter never reads the note's
sentences at all. The argv/toolpin/blobrun conjuncts genuinely bind the
*registry* row (improvement over round 90's `print` row), but the three
manifest rows — the routed items 1-2 this claim exists to close — are still
bound by token presence, the exact class QUALITY 141 names as the failure
mode. Against the rounds-81-82 precedents this is bookkeeping: criterion 78
makes the register a measured population, but a population measured by
substring presence measures entries, not repairs.

Worth recording as a tradeoff that does NOT save this counter: a human
reader *can* tell reject/falsereason from pos at a glance. But the frozen
predicate `ok = all(conj.values())` is the counter's own output, and the
kill rule is written against the counter, not against human intuition.
The cheapest safe correction (not applied — frozen artefacts are never edited
here): a `doc-contains` row whose tokens appear must additionally satisfy a
negation/quote screen, or the section must be checked for the operative
sentence pattern (`This row carries **not admitted**:` as a sentence head,
not after a rejection frame) and the coverage citation must be checked as a
citation (not named under a "does NOT cite" frame) — i.e. close the
`receipt-tokens`/`doc-contains` operative-sentence gap that QUALITY 141
already declares. Minimum: add a pre-freeze control carrying the required
tokens only inside a rejected quote; it must print rows open, or the claim
does not hold.
