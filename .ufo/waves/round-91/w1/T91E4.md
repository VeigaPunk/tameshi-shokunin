## Interpretation
Both clauses of H hold. (a) The `replace-refs-present` guard fires and exits 2
before any spec/gate read (phase 1 output contains only the two setup lines,
no `C1_ROW` at all). (b) The sealed environment inside the counter overrides
caller pollution: all 12 derived verdicts equal the raw-environment baseline
byte-for-byte. Limitation (declared by the plan, not a probe gap): the canary
shows the copy runs but cannot show which selftest arm executed; the probe
does not check the fleet-side guard (fleet `replace -l` was empty).
