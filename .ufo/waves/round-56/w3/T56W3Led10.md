The verdict is inherited from a carried-forward entry (the round-17 `gap-minor` entry at read_rev `eb7838b1…`), not from the report.

Impact on the frozen counter: none (`gap-minor` and `verified-record` are both in `REC_VERDICTS`; `entry_reason` returns `None` either way). Impact on the claim: the entry does not say what its named report says.
Fix sketch (not applied): take `verdict` from the report `d['verdict']` when present, and derive `newest` by round or by ancestry rather than by string max of `read_rev`.
