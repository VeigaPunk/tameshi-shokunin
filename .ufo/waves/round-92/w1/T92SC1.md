## 5 — A second-order hazard the draft had to be shaped around

The draft's first version opened with its own `source:` line, mirroring the
note's front-matter. That breaks the note's **own documented reproduce path**,
which the round-90 counter serves:

```

measure.py:233  cited = set(re.findall(r"\bS(\d{3,})\b", src[0])) if len(src) == 1 else set()

`len(src) == 1` — a second `source:` line empties `cited` and drops the
counter's `source` conjunct. Measured by `T92SC1-source-line-probe.sh` (the
round-90 counter over three arms of the note, scratch clones):

certificate.
