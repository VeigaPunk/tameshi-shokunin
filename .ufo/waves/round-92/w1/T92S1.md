| quote (as the lesson's Gate records it) | line in my stripped text | line in committed `s320.txt` | `wc -w` (whitespace tokens) | letter/digit words |

| "The sums are computed as described in FIPS-180-2." | 53 | 53 | **8** | 10 |

Both quotes are present verbatim in the live page (raw HTML `<p class="Pp">`
at source lines 88 and 127), at the line numbers the lesson cites
(`sources/s320.txt:19` and `:53`). Both are ≤ 25 whitespace tokens (6 and 8),
so the criterion-87 bar is satisfied on the measured quantity.

**Record defect (non-kill, append-only).** The lesson's Gate row labels these
`7 words` and `10 words`. Those are the **letter-and-digit** counts, not the
whitespace-token counts the accepting counter measures. QUALITY criterion 87
("a quote's length is measured, never written by hand") requires the label to
be the count the bar measures and states that "a letter-and-digit word count
is a second number and never a substitute for it". The correct labels for the
quoted strings are **6** and **8** whitespace tokens. The correction is
append-only: an errata line in `corpus/lessons/round-92.md` naming the
measured values (6 and 8), leaving the quotes, their line numbers and the
row's `teaches` cell untouched. Note the lane has one precedent of the same
shape — `corpus/lessons/round-79.md` labels "Recurse into sub-trees." 4 words
(measured: 3 whitespace tokens) — so the convention is whitespace tokens and
the round-92 labels are outliers. Neither label is a claim predicate: the
counter's `source` conjunct tests only that the row's status is `mined`, that
the used-by cell names the lesson, and that the lesson names S320 and the URL
(`measure-v92.py:313-324`); no conjunct reads the word count.
