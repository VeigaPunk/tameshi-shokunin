## Coverage finding (survives, reported not fixed)

`^\s*` also matches newlines under `re.M`, so an indented row separated from the previous row by a blank line still counts:

```

tab_indented            IND= ['S001', 'S002'] C0= ['S001']
no_trailing_space_row   IND= ['S001']          C0= ['S001']
```

The single-indent-line fixture cannot distinguish tab-indentation or blank-line-separated rows; a row written `|  S002 |` (two spaces in the cell) is missed by both extracts. Not a contract change.
