re-commit the instrument revision; the five states themselves need no change.

**F2 — LOW, wording precision, no claim impact.** Plan §A1 says "the four-column variant
does not discriminate". That holds for the construction A1 actually describes (witness cell
ending `C:\\` before a *space-separated* delimiter); a boundary-adjacent four-column variant
(`| C01 | fixture arm | C:\\| KILLED |`) does discriminate — my pre-amendment run printed
exit 1, `ids=false`, `width:9:cells=3` (`R95R1-before.out` Appendix A). The amended
five-column fixture is strictly better and both counters behave as declared; no change to
the claim or the fixture is needed, but the sentence over-generalises.

**F3 — INFO, declared limits verified.** An indented verdict line that continues a list item
is keyed by v95, which is the plan's stated limit; the committed note's last list item is
closed by its indent-2 continuation line, so the battery's appended indent fixtures stay
literal (verified against the battery's exact construction).

**F4 — GAP, not a falsification.** `.ufo/waves/round-95/claims.json` does not exist at
`0685c358`, so C1 kill rule (c)'s declared blob-vs-claims comparison cannot be run yet (the
counter blob at the revision is `5f0d1770…`). The frozen battery line
