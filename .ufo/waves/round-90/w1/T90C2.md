## 1. Question 1 — calibration move, or bookkeeping?

**Answer: a real record repair, but not a calibration measurement.** The lane's two admitted
precedents do not carry C2 as worded.

Admitted precedents (fleet `MANIFEST.json`, `lane:"tameshi"`, `type:"closure"`):

  `closure-defect bind (tools/defect-bind.py, five row kinds over committed bytes; 6/6 rows

  ("6 rows with identical id/kind/expect/tokens/paths at the declaration and the wave commit"),

work and no new row kind: its five rows are four `doc-contains` / one `receipt-tokens` substring
checks plus one exit-code + `stdout_contains` reader. That is the class the lane's own criteria
reject:

* `corpus/QUALITY.md` **136**: a counter that certifies a subject *did* work "binds to what an
  observer outside the subject recorded, not to anything the subject prints"; its battery must
  include a forgery that prints every parsed figure while doing nothing.

* `corpus/lessons/round-89-verifier-spotcheck.md` §3 already **rejected** "another print-binding
  counter" as a claim candidate, citing round-88's `executed-set-check.py` printing
  `executed_pass=11` while executing nothing.

A do-nothing control cannot reach PASS — the pre-freeze `C2-nowork-base`/`C2-nowork-instr` are
red, and my honest control below is green — so the counter discriminates "no repair bytes" from
"repair bytes". But that is the floor, not calibration: the same counter passes a state whose
"repair bytes" are a rejected quote and a `print`. Under the r81/r82 admission pattern (the
instrument must have shown the refusal it claims), **C2 is bookkeeping**: five token-bearing

The four repairs are real in the honest construction (my `honest` control, §2). The defect is not

## 3. Why this is a defect, not a declared tradeoff

* Rule (a) is satisfied literally: a **committed state** (scratch clone, any seat's — the rule's
  wording) reaches PASS while a **non-author** reading (this seat) finds items 2 and 4 not
  repaired. Two independent states, each a one-file diff from the honest construct.
* The counter's own described conjunct is falsified: `plan.md:87` and `measure-c2.sh`'s header
  both say the registry conjunct means "the row executes the real tool's selftest"; K1 prints
  `held=1/1` while the row executes a `print`. A conjunct that prints its label but not its
  condition is the exact M1 (round 88) / M7 (round 89) class.
* It is **fixable at low cost**, so it is not a limit:
  - row-level: bind `source` to `argv` (`source.split("@")[0] in argv`) and/or require the tool
    blob at `REV` to hash to a pinned sha; run the row and compare its stdout to the tool's own
    `--selftest` stdout (the honest row's stdout is the 21-arm output, not only the final line).
  - doc-level: make the row assert the correction, not a substring — a `required_sentence`
    (whitespace-normalized) plus a forbidden-assertion (`the kill and four other mistakes
    became lessons`/`the corrected count is ... four`) so a rejected quote cannot close it.
  - counter-level: pin `tools/defect-bind.py` and `tools/registry-check.py` at the freeze/base
    blob shas (or run the counter's own copies), and commit a print-stub tool control that must
    be refused; that is the K3 refusal QUALITY 138 asks for.
* The honest state at `99115edd` shows the four repairs themselves are real. The kill is of the
  predicate, not of an honest landing. That distinction matters for disposition (below) but does
  not soften the rule: the frozen counter cannot tell the two apart, so `C2_R90_PASS=true` is not
  evidence that the routed defects are closed.

## 5. Smallest safe correction and disposition

**Given the freeze**, the counter's sha is pinned (`plan.md:81`), so changing it after the
pre-freeze controls and the disclosed-data note would be a post-data narrowing. The smallest safe
disposition:

1. **Do not admit C2 as a calibration claim.** Record the kill under rule (a) with K1/K2 as the
   counterexamples.
2. **Land the four record repairs status-neutral** if the L1 wants them in the tree: they are real
   in the honest construction (`99115edd`, PASS) and fix genuine records (coverage-map row,
   entry citation, lesson corrections, instrument registry row). But the honest bytes alone do
   not make C2's claim: land them as record work, with the C2 predicate reported as token
   bookkeeping.
3. **Route the successor counter** (next round, pre-registered) with: `source`↔`argv` binding +
   pinned tool blob for the registry row; a `required_sentence`/forbidden-assertion row kind for
   the correction; and pinned blob shas for `defect-bind.py`/`registry-check.py` plus a
   print-stub tool control. The K1/K2/K3 fixtures here are directly reusable as that counter's
   negative controls.

The exact edits the counters need are in §3. No lane or fleet file was modified; the only files
