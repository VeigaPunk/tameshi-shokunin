**Answer to the target question: the sweep does not false-green on a trivial
regex — it refuses.** `extract_token` raises `figure-not-unique` on >1 match
(fail-closed, zero rows emitted); a loose regex dies before any verdict. This
is the r75 control-4 hazard closed by construction, not by regex strength.

Tightness argument, both sides. *For tighter (e.g. `PASS \(\d+/\d+\)`):*
binds the tally with its printed carrier, immune to a future doc cell that
gains a second bare `d/d` token. *Against:* the base before-cells are
`→ 18/18 PASS0` (row 244) and `→ PASS34/34 exit0` (row 246) — no `PASS (…)`
carrier — so the tighter regex turns the frozen before-state into
`figure-absent` refusals (exit 2), destroying the do-nothing control
(`unexpected=2`, exit 1) and breaching kill rule (i) (landed rows must equal
the frozen table). **Verdict:** `\d+/\d+` is the tightest form common to
`18/18 PASS0`, `PASS34/34 exit0` and `SELFTEST: PASS (19/19)`/`(66/66)`; the
frozen regex + uniqueness guard is correct. Residual hazard of wrong-token
binding requires a regex that matches exactly once on BOTH sides with equal
non-tally values — none constructible from the pinned stdout bytes (the only
`d/d` on either side is the tally; uniqueness verified by every successful
extraction above).
