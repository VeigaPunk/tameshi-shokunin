1616: ### Criterion 107 — a certified receipt names the revision it was measured against, and the gate refuses the ones that do not
1617: 
1618: Every receipt a round certifies — a control, a positive control or a verdict — states in its
1619: own bytes the revision it was measured against (`rev=<sha>` or `"rev": "<sha>"`), and the lane's
1620: certification path runs `tools/receipt-revision-gate.py` over it. A receipt whose named revision
1621: is content-identical to the certified revision on the surface the round declares with `--paths`
1622: is accepted even when the two shas differ (the surface did not move); a receipt whose named
1623: revision differs on that surface is refused `RECEIPT_REV_CONTENT_DRIFT`, one whose `rev=` does
1624: not resolve in the lane is refused `RECEIPT_REV_UNRESOLVABLE`, one that names no revision at all
1625: is refused `RECEIPT_REV_ABSENT`, and a receipt path that does not exist is refused
1626: `RECEIPT_MISSING`. The rule is not satisfied by a record that cites a receipt in prose: the
1627: receipt is refused or accepted on its own bytes, and a round that lands this rule also lands the
1628: refusal of a receipt the lane already accepted. Distinct from criterion 86, which governs *when*
1629: the content revision C is designated and what may lie between C and the landing tip; this
1630: criterion governs the receipt's own binding, is enforced by a named tool with closed codes, and
1631: is exercised on receipts accepted before the gate existed.
