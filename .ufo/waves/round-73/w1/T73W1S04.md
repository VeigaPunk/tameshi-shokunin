## 3. Findings

**F1 — deliverable.** The draft exists at the seat path with the three criteria
in house form (numbered `###` heading, normative sentence, `Lesson:` naming
`corpus/lessons/round-72-verifier-spotcheck.md` and the class, `Falsifier that
must fail:` with two commands and pasted output), plus the one-sentence wording
note per criterion. `corpus/QUALITY.md` was not edited.

**F2 — the landed criteria and this draft agree in class, differ in pinning.**
The landed 121/122/123 (QUALITY.md `:1971/:1998/:2020`) name the same classes
(`record-runs-ahead-of-artifacts`, `stale-self-description`,
`fixture-only-mechanism`) and the same lesson. The draft's differences are
scoped to the falsifiers: (a) 121 reads the wave artifacts at `9b4ffe47` rather
than at `a3dd3574` and adds the `w1-results.json` count (`seats=12 results=12`);
(b) 122 derives the green-row count from the manifest diff over the round-72
range (`f20ba48`→`9b4ffe47` → `4`) instead of matching `r72`/`s303` id
substrings; (c) 123 runs the census against a git-archive extraction of
`9b4ffe47` instead of the mutable lane worktree.

**F3 — the landed 122 falsifier no longer demonstrates its class on today's
bytes.** Its two halves were `python3 -c "… 'r72' in r['id'] or 's303' in
r['id'] …"` → `4` and `grep -c 'adds three green arms'
tools/pointer-recheck.json` → `1`. After the round-73 correction the second
grep reads `0` (measured at `1d118c14`; at the final read `8f7ff279`,
`git show HEAD:tools/pointer-recheck.json | grep -c 'adds four green arms'` →
`1` and `… 'adds three green arms'` → `0`), so the contradiction exists only at
`9b4ffe47`. The draft's pinned form reads `three` vs `4` on those bytes and
would keep doing so on any later tree.

**F4 — the landed 123 falsifier is not revision-stable, and the live registry
moved under it during this seat.** The landed falsifier reads the lane worktree;
its reading changed while this seat held the lane. Pinned to the round's own
record-edits commit, the registry was malformed:
