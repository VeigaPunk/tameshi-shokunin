Seat: scout, round 73 wave 1 (tameshi). Assignment: sweep the lane's and fleet's real
committed round records (fleet `tameshi.md` for rounds 60–73; lane
`.ufo/waves/round-*/w?-results.json` + spec files) and build the per-round table the L1

Deliverable table: `.ufo/waves/round-73/w1/T73W1S06/recon-table.md`.

## Method

* All artifacts read from git objects at a named revision
  (`git cat-file blob <rev>:<path>`, `git ls-tree -r <rev> -- <dir>`) — never from a
  worktree, so untracked in-flight round-73 files cannot leak into a row.
* Numeric claims enumerated by a fixed pattern set (`N seats`, `N seat reports`,
  `N reports`, `N/M seats`, `seats=`/`reports=`, `settled N/M`, `N settled`,
  `N cap-cancelled`, `N seat records`) plus a spelled-out-number scan
  (`one…sixteen`, `thirty-two` + `seat|report`). Every enumerated span was then
  re-read by direct byte slicing of the blob to confirm the offset pair.
* Seat counts cross-read three ways where available: `results.seats`,
  `len(results)`, committed top-level `*.md` under the wave dir, plus (unused by the
  instrument) `w1-dispatch.json` `counts.total`.
* Future-row viability was measured, not asserted: a scratch manifest
  (`/fast/scratch/hyaku/tameshi-r73/T73W1S06/probe-r60.json`, `…/probe-r73.json`)
  was fed to the real `tools/wave-reconcile.py` with `--repo . --extra-root
  fleet=/home/vgpnk/Projects/shokunin`. No repo file was written.

## Instrument gaps this sweep surfaced (for the L1's manifest design, no edit made)

1. **`seats` cannot distinguish dispatched from returned.** On r60 it reads
   `printed=32 seats=32 records=32` → meet, although only 2 of the 32 seats ever ran
   (`settled=2`, 30 `note`-only records, 2 committed seat reports). A r60 `seats` row is
   green for the wrong reason; only `seat_reports` (or an unbuilt `settled` kind) sees it.
2. **No `dispatch` row kind.** `w1-dispatch.json counts.total` (32 / 12 / 13) is a third
   independent seat-count source, committed at the same pins, and nothing compares it.
3. **Spec filename is not uniform.** `spec-w1.json` (r60) vs `w1-spec.json` (r72, r73);
   a manifest generator keyed on `w?-spec.json` silently loses r60.
4. **Results schema drifted.** r60 carries `settled` + `dead_or_never_spawned` keys and
   `agent="task"` for every record; r72/r73-candidate carry real role names and neither
   key. A `roles`/`attack_share` row over r60 is meaningless.
5. **Claim grammar is narrow.** Word-numbered claims (`three seats`) and
   "N settled" style counts have no row kind; only `N reports` / `N seats` bind.

## Remaining unknowns / limits (honest)

* The sweep enumerates claims matching the stated pattern set; a seat/report count
  phrased outside it (e.g. "a dozen seats", "the full roster") would not be listed.
  The spelled-out scan covered one–sixteen and thirty-two with zero hits outside r72's
  two "three seats" phrases.
* Rounds 45–59 waves exist under the same lane path but are out of the assigned 60–73
  scope and were not swept.
* Non-seat numeric claims in the reports (ledger counts, section counts, receipt
  counts) were not reconciled — out of scope for this seat.
* Round 73's eventual `w1-results.json` cannot be predicted; every r73 statement here
  is about the tree as of `17cd1939`/HEAD at scan time.
