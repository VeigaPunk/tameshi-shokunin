Reviewed both directions:

1. **Counter-side (expectation)**: expected map `missing→1/DRIFT` matches the
   contract exit_map verbatim ("a declared source differs or is **missing**/nonregular

   owned cell is coincidentally robust to that bug class; the cells that would

   Conversely a tool mapping "blob lookup failed" to exit 2 would fail this
   cell — the oracle shows the correct folding is DRIFT(1), since the trusted
