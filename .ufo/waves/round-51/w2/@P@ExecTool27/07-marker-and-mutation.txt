I4. verdict on marker meaningfulness
  The marker is COMPUTED (bytes_unflagged/replace_unflagged/bytes_flagged/replace_flagged/scratch_replace counts)
  and moves with the detector: neutering EXPECTED_RE -> bytes/replace_unflagged drop to 0 and exit 1;
  neutering FLAG_RE -> bytes/replace_flagged rise to 1 and exit 1. So it is a real diagnostic, not help text.
  CAVEAT: neutering SCRATCH_RE alone does NOT move the verdict (scratch_replace is an EXPECTED_RE-negative
  line, so it can never become unflagged regardless of SCRATCH_RE) -> that one of the five counters is vacuous.
