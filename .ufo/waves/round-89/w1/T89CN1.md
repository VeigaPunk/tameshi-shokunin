- **(A)** other lanes' counters or gates whose *predicate reads a figure the subject prints*
  — the class round 89 closes for tameshi's `tools/mutate-and-run.sh` ledger.

  rather than the subject's own report.

## Findings

1. **Class (A) is not unique to tameshi.** At least six sibling lanes carry a counter or
   gate whose deciding term is a figure the certified subject prints: kotoba's assertion
   floors (#1) and mutation battery (#2), kumitate's K43 (#3) and PASS_STUB selftest (#7),
   tabi's kill rule (#4), tsukuroi's r83 kill (#5) and tally sweep (#8), and chousa's
   capture-line count (#10). Two of them (tsukuroi r83, kumitate/tabi exit-code terms)
   already bind the *exit status*, which is kernel-authored; none binds *which bytes ran*
   or *what the process opened*. That is the residual the witness adds.
2. **Class (B) already exists in five lanes, with narrower objects.** strace is used for:
   env/argv capture (tsukuroi #12), opened-path truth (kake #13), absence of extra execs
   (kaidoku #14), path-input receipts (tameshi #11); ptrace/procfs for code identity and
   mapping (kama #15, #16). **No sibling tool binds an arbitrary subject's declared ledger
   to kernel events.** The nearest thing to a portable witness is kake's `open_expectation`
   (a per-fixture declared expectation checked against an `openat` trace) — that is the
   witness pattern at fixture scale, not at runner scale.
3. **Two sibling instruments already declare the exact gap W_ORDER closes.** kotodama
   (#9) says hash equality proves preservation, not absence of writes; kotoba (#1) says a
   self-test that loses assertions must not read green but can only compare a scalar. Both
   are natural downstream consumers of a kernel-observed counter, and both would need the
   witness's per-leg identity (not a scalar) to adopt it.
4. **One hard conflict for generalization (#16).** kama's round-64 counter's frozen
   predicate includes `tracer_pid == 0`. Any attempt to certify that counter with an
   strace-based witness fails the predicate it is trying to certify. The witness pattern
   must either (a) exclude "not traced" predicates, or (b) use an observation channel that
   does not set `TracerPid` (procfs sampling + fanotify/eBPF need privileges the lane does
   not have). Naming this in the round's limits is cheaper than discovering it in a port.
5. **Nested tracing is a second-order risk (#14, #12).** kaidoku's battery already traces
   each invocation; wrapping it in the witness yields strace-under-strace, and the inner
   `strace` binary's own execve becomes an extra exec from the outer trace's point of
   view (the same attribution problem tsukuroi r36 solved for wrapper/shebang execs).
   The witness's W_EXTRA/W_EXEC accounting must exclude the audited tracer, or a port on
   that lane returns a false finding.
6. **Byte-figure counters are out of scope by construction (#8, #10).** Comparing two
   printed figures (report vs receipt, capture line count) cannot be upgraded by kernel
   observation; the honest repair is a per-item declared ledger (the witness's W_PARSE +
   W_MAT), not a trace.

## Risks and limits of this scan

- Static grep only; no sibling tool was executed. Line numbers are as of this scan and
  sibling lanes are live (they move under this seat's feet).
- "Applicability" is a design judgment derived from each tool's own docstring/predicate,
  not a measurement; a port must re-derive the predicate from the lane's frozen plan.
- Scope excluded corpus, LOG/SCORECARD, plans and `.ufo/` evidence, so gates implemented
  as inline shell inside those files are not enumerated. A follow-up sweep over
  `SKILL.md` gate tables is the obvious next check if the round wants coverage rather
  than adjacency.
- The scan did not test whether any sibling gate *already* fails under tracing; only
  kama round-64's predicate states it.
