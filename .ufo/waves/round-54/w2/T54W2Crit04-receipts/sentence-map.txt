01 cites=- | # The scratch-root contract — three arms, one exit class (round 54)
02 cites=- | 
03 cites=- | Every lane tool that allocates scratch through a `strict_scratch_root()` helper
04 cites=- | shares one contract. Before round 53 the helpers were seven near-copies of the
05 cites=- | same fifteen lines and only some of their callers mapped the failure to the
06 cites=- | documented class; round 54 makes the mapping uniform and this note is the rule
07 cites=- | the seven tools and their registry rows now name.
08 cites=- | 
09 cites=- | ## Three arms
10 cites=- | 
11 cites=- | | Arm | `TMPDIR` | Behaviour | Exit class |
12 cites=- | |---|---|---|---|
13 cites=- | | unset | absent | the platform default (`tempfile.gettempdir()`) is used | — |
14 cites=['S278'] | | empty | `""` | **unset**: Python's resolver skips an empty candidate, so the next candidate wins (S278) and the platform default applies | — |
15 cites=- | | unusable | a path that is not a writable directory | the tool refuses before it allocates: the value is named, the class is printed, nothing falls back to `/tmp` | **2, could-not-evaluate** |
16 cites=- | 
17 cites=- | The empty arm is not a convenience. The reference implementation reads
18 cites=- | `for envname in 'TMPDIR', 'TEMP', 'TMP': dirname = _os.getenv(envname); if dirname:
19 cites=['S278'] | dirlist.append(dirname)` (S278 `_candidate_tempdir_list`:163-165), so an empty
20 cites=- | `TMPDIR` never becomes a candidate directory; the documented list also starts at
21 cites=- | "the directory named by the TMPDIR environment variable" and then falls through
22 cites=['S279'] | (S279). A tool that treated `""` as a path would try to allocate under the
23 cites=- | working directory — a silent, wrong scratch root — which is why the arm is
24 cites=- | declared rather than left to whatever `os.path.isdir("")` happens to return.
25 cites=- | 
26 cites=- | The standard fixes only the variable's meaning, not its empty-string handling:
27 cites=- | `TMPDIR` "shall represent a pathname of a directory made available for programs
28 cites=['S277'] | that need a place to create temporary files" (S277). The empty arm is therefore
29 cites=- | a lane declaration that follows the resolver the lane actually calls, and the
30 cites=- | registry rows say so.
31 cites=- | 
32 cites=- | ## Why the class is uniform
33 cites=- | 
34 cites=- | A set-but-unusable `TMPDIR` is an environment fact, not a failed measurement: the
35 cites=- | gate did not run, so no verdict may be printed and the exit class is
36 cites=- | could-not-evaluate (2), never 1 (a verdict of "differs"/"failed") and never a
37 cites=- | traceback. Round 53 removed the silent `/tmp` fallback in seven tools and its
38 cites=- | own closure spot-check routed the residue: four of the seven still raised an
39 cites=- | uncaught `RuntimeError` from the self-test path (rc 1 with a traceback of the
40 cites=- | message), so the class existed in three tools and not in four (routed defect
41 cites=- | R53.6). Round 54 gives each of the four a `ScratchRootUnusable(RuntimeError)`
42 cites=- | subclass raised by the helper and caught at the entry point, printing one line
43 cites=- | that names the value and the class.
44 cites=- | 
45 cites=- | ## What measures it
46 cites=- | 
47 cites=- | `python3 .ufo/evidence/round-54/c2/measure.py` runs each of the seven tools'
48 cites=- | self-tests under three `TMPDIR` states (unusable, empty, usable) and reads the
49 cites=- | registry rows: 7/7 exit 2 with the naming line and no traceback, 7/7 exit 0 on
50 cites=- | the empty arm, 7/7 exit 0 on a usable root with no `mkdir` under `/tmp` under
51 cites=- | `strace`, and 7/7 rows carrying the `scratch_root` cell. The counter's blind
52 cites=- | spot is stated in the contract: it sees the self-test path, not every branch a
53 cites=- | tool might use to allocate scratch.
54 cites=- | 
55 cites=- | ## Falsifier
56 cites=- | 
57 cites=- | A tool that returns 0 after printing the could-not-evaluate line (control
58 cites=- | CTL_EXIT0), or that names the class without the value, fails the counter. A
59 cites=- | tool that regains a `dir="/tmp"` literal fails the round-53 monitor and the C2
60 cites=- | `tmp_mkdirs` arm together.
