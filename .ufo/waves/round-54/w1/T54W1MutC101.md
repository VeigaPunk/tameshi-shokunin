the readers failure is redundant with other failing arms. The bounded detector

either way. That is a coverage finding against the claim's kill rule that

arm4 itself is load-bearing.

flips `C1_R54_PASS` from true to false — without that CTL, deleting the
`reader_ok != len(frozen)` failure survives every run that already fails
precheck/monitor/rekey.
