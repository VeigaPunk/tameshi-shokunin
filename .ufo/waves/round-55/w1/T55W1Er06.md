## 3. The rule broken

**Primary — QUALITY 77** (`corpus/QUALITY.md:916`), quoted byte-exact:

> A seat's report is lane evidence: it is written under the lane's own wave directory, and a
> lane's own count of its residual reports must match the files that are actually committed
> there.

The seat's report and two of its cited receipts were lane evidence that the lane did not
carry committed at closure.

**Secondary — the round's own precondition** (`deciding-run.sh:8`): "the working tree must be
clean". It was tested once, at 12:22:17, and never again; the 12:25:37 witness recorded the
violation and `DECIDING_RUN_EXIT=0` did not read it. A snapshot is not an invariant: any file
written during the invocation escapes the check.

## 6. Observation about the frozen counter (reported, not fixed)

`.ufo/evidence/round-55/c1/measure.py:205-211` implements R54.6 as:

```python
    swept = (…three paths…)
```

stale occurrences of the wrong assertion outside the corrections section") is **vacuous for

the corrections section is measured. This matches the tree (I found no false prose sentence

at base — grep for `Sent03|R54.6|ff2a821` over the three files returns nothing), so I do not
claim the counter is wrong; I flag that the contract sentence is stronger than the instrument
for R54.6. Frozen — not edited.
