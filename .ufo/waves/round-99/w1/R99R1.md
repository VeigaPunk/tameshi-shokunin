## Findings

F1 — grading sentence over-claims (falsifies-wording; kill rule (a) shape). The wording says "every line of the receipt carrying the claimed token … shows the claimed N/M". The grader only sees lines ending in `pass` (`TOKEN_RE` and `RECEIPT_SELFTEST_RE` both require `\s+pass\b`). A receipt line `D_SELFTEST: 9/9 fail` beside `D_SELFTEST: 8/8 pass` is ignored, and the pair grades `matched`:
  `TALLY matched p/round-3.md:1 token D_SELFTEST claimed=8/8 receipt=.ufo/evidence/round-3/a.out observed=8/8 detail=equal`, rc 0 (`R99R1-attacks-batch5.txt`, case `gap-every-line-fail-suffix`). Same for `D_SELFTEST: 9/9` with no suffix (`gap-every-line-no-pass-suffix`), and for selftest-word form with `C_SELFTEST: 9/9 FAIL` (`gap-every-line-selftest-fail-suffix`, matched, rc 0).
  Impact on the frozen numbers: none observed. `git grep` over `.ufo/evidence/round-93`…`round-98` at `27a5e49e` finds token N/M lines and every one carries `pass` (`R99R1-receipt-grep-control.txt`: 17 receipts with pass-form lines; non-pass scan prints nothing, grep -v rc 1, git grep rc 0). So the frozen predicate is not moved, but the sentence claims more than the grader enforces. Narrowing that would make it true: "every line of the receipt carrying the claimed token in the `TOKEN: N/M pass` form".

F2 — citation grammar over-claims for scheme-less URLs (falsifies-wording against the docstring's "not part of a URL"). The only URL exclusion is `"://"` within 12 characters before the citation, or `https?://` anywhere before it. `www.h.example/x/a.out` and `h.example/x/a.out` are cited as paths and graded `unbound unresolved` (rc 1). `ftp://` and `HTTP://` are excluded. (`R99R1-attacks-batch2.txt` case `cite-url-www`, `cite-host-no-scheme`; `R99R1-attacks-batch5.txt` case `cite-www-no-scheme-with-token-match`.) Enforced property is narrower: "not after a `scheme://`". No frozen-record effect observed (no record cites a scheme-less host in these 12 records — [INFERENCE from the 12-record output above: no such `unbound` lines appear other than the basename/`selftest.txt` ones]).

F3 — citation grammar: suffix not anchored at path end (falsifies-wording against the docstring's "a path ending `.out`, `.txt`, `.log` or `.json`"). `CITE_RE` ends `\.(?:out|txt|log|json)\b`, so `a.out.bak` is cited as `a.out` and graded matched against `.ufo/evidence/round-3/a.out` (rc 0; `R99R1-attacks-batch5.txt` case `cite-out-bak-chain`). `a.outfile` and `a.json5` are correctly not cited (no word boundary). No effect on the frozen twelve (the 12-record output lists `selftest.txt` and `c2-selftest.out`, `c1-selftest.out`, `c2-selftest.out` only).

F4 — selftest form is not anchored to the word-form docstring in one respect: `selftest` followed by any non-digit run of up to 12 characters (including letters, colons, backticks) and then `N/M` counts as a tally with no `pass` requirement, while the token form needs `pass`. This matches the docstring ("the word `selftest` followed within 12 non-digit characters by `N/M`") and is neutral against the frozen sentence; recorded so the asymmetry is visible.

F5 — frozen-number and selftest sentences hold; no mismatch. Re-derivation at `27a5e49e` and `466510515` is identical to the frozen predicate; the red arm and 17/17 selftest match (`R99R1-frozen-numbers.txt`). The selftest's `token-mismatched-r94-shape` and `selftest-word-r98-d2-shape` arms are present and pass.

F6 — process: `.ufo/waves/round-99/records-rev.txt` is absent, so the wording's `R` is not yet a pinned commit. The frozen numbers hold at both revisions tested, so this is a pinning gap, not a falsification.

No repair was made. Wording narrowings for F1–F3 are for the L1 amendment step in plan §Amendments (pre-records-run), if the L1 chooses to narrow.
