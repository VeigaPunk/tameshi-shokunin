### 2. Two frozen-grammar probes on the committed bytes (both `neutral` for the frozen wording, and both already fixed by the L1's in-flight amendment)

Probed against the committed bytes at `46651051` (not the worktree), in a scratch fixture repo whose `git rev-parse --show-toplevel` was asserted to equal the fixture dir before its commit:

- **Citation suffix.** A record line citing `` `a.out.bak` `` was read by the frozen `CITE_RE` as a citation of `a.out` and graded `matched` — a path whose suffix is *not* a receipt suffix is treated as one, and the pair is graded against a different file than the record names. The frozen wording says the grammar is "a path ending `.out`/`.txt`/`.log`/`.json`", which the frozen `CITE_RE` (`[A-Za-z0-9_./-]+\.(?:out|txt|log|json)\b`) does not enforce. The L1's worktree amendment fixes this (`CITE_RE` now requires the suffix to end the path). Receipt `R99S1-frozen-grammar-probes.txt`.
- **Receipt-line suffix.** The frozen `RECEIPT_SELFTEST_RE` requires the literal ` pass` after `N/M`, so a receipt line `Z_SELFTEST: 9/9 fail` is invisible to the grader: the pair graded `matched` on the receipt's `8/8 pass` line even though the same receipt carries a contradicting `9/9` line. The frozen wording says a pair is `matched` "only when every line of the receipt carrying the claimed token (or the receipt's single `*_SELFTEST` token) shows the claimed N/M" — at the freeze, a contradicting line is not read at all. The L1's worktree amendment fixes this (the receipt-side regex no longer requires ` pass`). Receipt `R99S1-frozen-grammar-probes.txt`.

Both probes are `neutral` against the *frozen* wording in the sense that neither falsifies a frozen **number** and neither is a defect of the committed instrument's *published* behaviour in the twelve-record population (no receipt in that population carries a `fail` line, and no record line there cites a `.bak` path). They are reported because the wording's grammar description is stricter than the frozen code enforces, and because a knowledge entry that restates the grammar must not overstate it. My entry states the rule the instrument enforces and marks the two edges as the L1's amendment closes.
