### Criterion 128 — a settlement claim is measured at the revision it names, and the record never names a pre-settlement revision as the tip

A settlement claim — a rekey batch or a monitor re-read that restores
credit — is a measurement, and a measurement is evidence only at the
revision it was taken at. The credit a rekey restores is pinned to the
input hashes the rekey recorded, and every record commit after the rekey
re-drifts those inputs, so credit measured at a pre-record revision says
nothing about the tip the record ships at. A sentence that cites a
settlement receipt therefore carries three checkable fields: the receipt,
the revision the receipt measured, and the revision the sentence calls the
tip — and the last must be the tip of the range the record describes
(mechanically: `git rev-list --count <record tip> ^<named rev>` prints 0,
or the sentence explicitly labels the named revision historical and pins
the receipt that carries it). When further record commits land after a
rekey, the payment is a re-read at the new tip — rekeying the newly
uncredited set if the reading dropped — never a promotion of the old
receipt's revision to "tip" in prose. A tip claim that fails the count is
not a typo for the next round's margin: it is the record-runs-ahead-of-
artifacts class measured on the calibration axis, corrected append-only,
with the correcting sentence naming both revisions.

Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-76-verifier-spotcheck.md` §6 routed defect 1
and `corpus/lessons/round-77.md` `## Mistakes` (class
record-runs-ahead-of-artifacts): the round-76 settlement says "monitor
`credited=65/65` at the landing tip (`b2b0f82a`)" while `b2b0f82a` is the
last rekey batch — one commit before the sentence's own landing commit
`d08d5bee`, two before the round-76 record tip `d2d00e3f`, three before the
round-77 base `ac6baf7a` — and the monitor at the true tip reads 63/65.
R77-C2 is the payment: re-read at the tip, rekey exactly the measured
uncredited set, land the rekey as the tip.

Falsifier that must fail (revision-pinned: the sentence is superseded by
the round-77 append-only correction, so the contradiction exists at the
round-76 bytes and only there): `git -C
/home/vgpnk/Projects/shokunin/tameshi-shokunin show
ac6baf7a:corpus/lessons/round-76.md | grep -c 'at the landing tip
`b2b0f82a'` prints `1` while `git -C
/home/vgpnk/Projects/shokunin/tameshi-shokunin rev-list --count ac6baf7a
^b2b0f82a` prints `3` — the file carrying the sentence has moved three
