1. **Calibration (cross-claim C1), material.** The landing rev has no round-17 cold-read ledger
   (`git show eb7838…:.ufo/evidence/round-17/coldread/ledger.json` → `path … does not exist`).
   Therefore `measure.py --rev eb7838b1…` exits 0 but reports `ledger_present false`, `0/58`,
   `box3_full_page false`, score **6** — not `box3_full_page true`, score 8. I then proved the
   mechanism end-to-end inside the /tmp clone: assembled a report carrying this section's real
   entry (inputs + receipts) into `coldread/ledger.json`, committed ledger + receipts
   (`87d6779`), and ran strict `measure.py --rev 87d6779`: `ledger_present true`, `58/58`,
   `box3_full_page true`, score **8**, `overrides_used false`, `trace_gate strict`, my section
   `{"start":1897,"credited":true,"reason":"credited"}`. So C1 is achievable only at a rev that
   *contains* the ledger; the cited `--rev <landing>` form cannot show it at eb7838.
2. **Minor — "ledger missing → 2" is over-broad.** For the row's own `--rev` form, a missing
   committed ledger is exit 0 with `no-ledger` (observed: 58/58 no-ledger at eb7838); exit 2
   occurs only through the unlisted `--ledger-file`/`--ledger-rev` flags
   (`ledger-file-unreadable`, `ledger-blob-missing`). The error taxonomy exists, the wording
   does not cover the default path.
3. **Minor — selftest exit vocabulary.** `trace-inputs.py --selftest` is 0 (27/27) unwrapped but
   1 (4/27) under the cold-read interface's mandated wrapper, because this host forbids nested
   ptrace. Exit 1 appears nowhere in the row's 0/2/64. The tool is sound; the mandated
   verification path cannot observe it green.
4. **Operational note for the L1.** `measure.py`'s strict trace gate resolves `traces[]` as blobs
   *at REV*, so the assembled ledger and every referenced receipt must be committed together
   before the cited number is taken; uncommitted worktree receipts are invisible to it.
   `assemble.py`, in contrast, resolves receipts against the worktree.
5. **Note.** Receipts `20-measure-selfledger.json` and `21-trace-nostrace.json` were taken while
   the clone HEAD was the synthetic ledger commit `87d6779`; they are diagnostic-only and are
   excluded from the v2 `traces` list (a declared receipt with `rev != read_rev` is rejected
   `trace-rev-mismatch`). Receipt 22 repeats the strace-unavailable case at the read rev and is
   included.
