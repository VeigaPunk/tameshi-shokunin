## tools/conjunct-disposition.py  — closest LANE surface to C1 on shape; also C2's subject
- Header: "every conjunct key the frozen counter prints must have a manifest row that is
  either a committed battery state whose receipt shows that conjunct FALSE ... or a committed
  setup refusal whose receipt shows the named C1_SETUP token ... or {"unfalsifiable": ...}";
  "A manifest key the counter does not print is a finding".
- Reads: counter blob at `--rev`, `tools/conjunct-disposition.json`, receipts dir.
- Prints: `DISPOSITION rows=19 falsifiable=17 unfalsifiable=2 findings=0`; refuses
  `DISPOSITION_SETUP ...` exit 2; exit 1 on findings.
=> Expectations are STATIC manifest rows. C1's expectations are IMPORTED from the committed
   driver (`states()`, `EXPECT_EXTRA`, `grade()`), i.e. an executable oracle; the population
   is 50 battery receipts, not 19 conjunct rows. Not a duplicate.
=> The resident tool is the round-93 version (sha256 1b1c6895…); C2 measures the round-96
   evidence copy (sha256 02a5482a…). See receipt 05.

## tools/receipt-bytes-bind.py  — closest LANE surface to C1 on the binding axis
- Header: "a receipt's recorded tool sha must exist in the committed bytes of a revision the
