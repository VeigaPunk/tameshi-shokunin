## Findings — conflicts with committed bytes, reported verbatim

These are for the canonical judge. Each carries the command and the observed output.

**F1 — the three C1 body-edit receipts are the same bytes; two of the five registered
forgeries never reach the grader.** `c1-forge-counter-sha.out`, `c1-forge-exit.out`,
`c1-forge-payload.out` and `c1-forge-conjunct.out` all carry sha256
`1c9754f32a016b05c23f3e70736bf70af5084881760cddaaafe41c6be343b841` and all print
`BATTERY_ROW pos-real:MISMATCH(counter-sha)` — never `MISMATCH(exit=7)`, never the payload
failure. Cause, in the committed driver: `controls97.py:135,143` do `git reset -q --hard r`
where `r` is the **previous attack's** commit, so the `counter_sha=0…0` edit rides into the
`exit=` and payload trees, and the reader stops at its digest check
(`battery-bind.py:154`). My isolated re-runs (`SC1-isolated-forges.txt`, one edit at a time on
a pristine receipt) give `MISMATCH(exit=7)` for the exit edit and `MISMATCH(exit=0)` for the
payload edit, each `matched=49 mismatched=1 missing=0`, rc 1. **The claim is not falsified —
the wording's sentences hold — but the suite cannot tell an unreached edit from a killed one,
so two of the five registered refusals are not established by their receipts.** Both R97R1
(N1) and R97C1 (§1 item 1) found this independently; the L1's A3-A14 did not touch it, and
the landed Measurements section states "the five forgeries each move the line" without the
caveat. **Outstanding at the landing tip.**

**F2 — the lesson's Gate word counts are wrong in both citations.** The Gate's own convention
(line 13: "Word counts are `len(quote.split())`") gives **15** for the S159 span, not the
**14** printed at `corpus/lessons/round-97.md:19`; and **15** for the S006 located span, not
the **16** printed at line 28. Both are the QUALITY-172 defect (round-95 M1). The quotes and
their line spans are correct.

**F3 — both Gate HTTP dates contradict the committed headers.** The lesson prints
`08 Nov 2026 14:31:52 GMT` for S159; `headers-s159.txt` records
`date: Sun, 08 Nov 2026 04:11:08 GMT`. The lesson prints `14:18:19 GMT` for S006;
`headers-s006.txt` records `date: Sun, 08 Nov 2026 14:04:16 GMT`. Neither claimed date
appears in any committed file except the lesson itself.
